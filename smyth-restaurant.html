Our Philosophy
"Food is memory,
place, and
pure presence." — Chefs John Shields & Karen Urie Shields

At Smyth, every dish begins with a question: what is at its peak today? Our kitchen works intimately with a network of farmers, foragers, and artisan producers across the Midwest to source ingredients that are not merely fresh, but alive with flavor.

The tasting menu evolves with the seasons — sometimes weekly, sometimes nightly — so that each visit becomes its own singular chapter. Technique serves the ingredient, never the other way around.

This is food that remembers where it came from, and invites you to do the same.
